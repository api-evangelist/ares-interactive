Players Who Pre-Register for Ares Interactive’s and AMC Global Media’s Roguelite Will Receive a Free Survivor and Survival Supplies Cache We’re excited to announce that The Walking Dead: Aftermath, the first free-to-play roguelite game set in the universe of AMC’s smash hit television series The Walking Dead, is now open for pre-registration. Players who secure their spot before launch will add T-Dog to their roster, a rare gear piece, and additional resources. The game will launch on the App Store and Google Play in Summer 2026, with additional platforms to be announced at a later date.
